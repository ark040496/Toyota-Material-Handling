Toyota Material Handling

Re-engineering forklift discovery for fleet buyers.

I turned a high-friction legacy equipment catalog into an accessible digital showroom, a side-by-side spec comparator and a mobile resource for warehouse directors, fleet buyers and dealership teams.

Overview

What is the Toyota Material Handling catalog?

Toyota Material Handling leads North America in industrial lift trucks, order pickers and automated guided vehicles. Buyers use its digital catalog to specify equipment by aisle width, powertrain and load capacity before they call a dealer.

Problem statement

What was happening earlier?

The legacy experience was organised around Toyota’s internal taxonomy, not around how fleet teams specify equipment. When buyers couldn’t verify specs, 61% left to compare models on third-party sites.

Manuals 4+ clicks deepNo model comparison68% mobile bounceLow-contrast spec textRetyping specs into spreadsheetsCalling a rep for answers

Opportunity

Put structured specs, manuals and CAD files above the fold on every model page.

Let buyers compare up to three models side by side without leaving the site, on any device.

Research and discovery

Grounding discovery in warehouse and dealership reality

I ran quantitative funnel surveys alongside moderated contextual interviews with warehouse logistics managers, forklift fleet operators and regional dealership sales engineers.

61%

of B2B buyers left Toyota’s site to compare models elsewhere, so side-by-side comparison became the central design goal.

Key insights

01

Specs and manuals were buried

Mast height, turning radius, load centre and OSHA checklists sat in unindexed PDFs four levels deep.

02

Comparison happened off-site

Fleet managers opened many tabs and retyped specs into spreadsheets.

03

Mobile was an afterthought

42% of traffic was mobile, but fixed-width tables and tiny tap targets drove a 68% bounce rate.

04

Contrast failed in the field

Grey-on-grey spec text failed contrast thresholds in high-glare warehouse lighting.

    Design decisions and impact

    Design decisions that replaced tab-hopping with one comparison flow

    • Surfaced structured spec ledgers and one-click PDF and CAD downloads above the fold.
    • Built an inline three-model matrix covering load capacity, turn radius and powertrain cost of ownership.
    • Moved to a mobile-first architecture with thumb-reachable filter drawers and instant dealer quote triggers.
    • Set a WCAG 2.2 AA token set with 17.8:1 chassis-ink contrast and full keyboard operation.
