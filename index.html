Buyers had to hunt through hierarchies built around Toyota, not around them

Toyota leads North America in lift trucks, order pickers and automated guided vehicles. Our UX audit and analytics review showed a catalog organised by internal taxonomy, not by how warehouse directors and procurement teams specify equipment.

When buyers couldn’t verify aisle width, powertrain or side-by-side capacity, 61% left to compare specs on third-party sites.
